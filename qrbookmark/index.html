Why four panels and not one. A single QR code caps at 2,331 bytes of error-corrected byte data, and filling the card width with one symbol would put the modules at 0.32 mm — smaller than most printers hold cleanly. Four stacked panels keep the modules large enough to survive paper and a phone camera, and the parity panel buys tear tolerance on top of each symbol's own error correction.
Reading a card. Scan with the companion QR Bookmark Reader app, which crops and reads all four panels automatically — or tap any panel's link with a phone's stock camera and no app at all; opening three of the four assembles the text the same way.
