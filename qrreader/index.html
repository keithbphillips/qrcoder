Scan a card / read it

Point your camera at a QR Bookmark card — the four panels scan automatically, and any three of the four rebuild the whole excerpt. Recovered text opens straight into a full-screen reader with adjustable font, size, and light or dark mode. Once scanned, a book is saved on this device so you never have to scan it again.
